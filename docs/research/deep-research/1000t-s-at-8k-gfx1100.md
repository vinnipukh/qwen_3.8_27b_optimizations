## Executive Summary

**1000 tok/s at 8k is the worst possible cliff to chase on a 20 GB consumer card, and every ingredient to hit it is still experimental on gfx1100.**

*   **Prefill is quadratic, not linear.** Attention FLOPs are `O(N²·d)` (`N=seq_len`). At 8k the `QKᵀ` matrix is `8192²=67M` elements/head (`~134 MB FP16`), at 32k it is `1B` (`2 GB`) — `16×` more work, `16×` more HBM writes unless fused. `FlashAttention-1` makes HBM `O(N)` via SRAM tiling (2–4× over unfused, but only 25–40% of GEMM peak) and `FlashAttention-2` fixes work-partitioning to reach **50–73% of GEMM, 225 TFLOP/s (72% MFU) on A100** [arxiv 2307.08691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08691) [arxiv 2205.14135](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14135). Even fused, compute stays quadratic — `tok/s = N / time(N)` → `~1/N` without Flash, `~2×/4×` latency for `8k→16k→32k` *with* Flash. You still pay the `N²` FMAs.
*   **KV alone eats the card at 8k.** Stored `per_token = 2·(K+V)·2B·layers·kv_heads·head_dim`. HF's `kv-cache-quantization` blog shows `Llama-2 7B` (`32L/32H/128`) ⇒ `10k tokens → 5 GB ⇒ 512 KiB/tok` [huggingface.co/blog/kv-cache-quantization](https://huggingface.co/blog/kv-cache-quantization). For GQA (`32 Q / 8 KV heads`, what Qwen-32B documents as `64 Q /8 KV, 64 layers` [huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-32B](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-32B)) the math collapses to `~128 KiB/tok` fp16, `32–128 KiB` int4 — so `8k·128KiB≈1 GB`, `8k·512KiB≈4 GB`. With the locked `IQ4_XS` model at `15.3 GB` plus `8k KV ~3.2 GB` (≈`32 layers×...) you are at `18.5 GB` leaving `<1.5 GB` headroom in `20 GB`. At `32k` KV is `≈12.8 GB` ⇒ `28 GB >20 GB` guaranteed OOM.
*   **RDNA3 has the flops, but not the fused path on WSL2.** `RX 7900 XTX` is `96 CU, 61.4 TFLOP/s FP32, 122.8 TFLOP/s FP16, 960 GB/s` (`7900 XT: 800 GB/s`), `96 MB` L3, `5.3 TB/s` intra-die Fanout [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RDNA_3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RDNA_3), with **wave-cooperative WMMA** (`16×16×16`, `wave32` native, `INT4/INT8/FP16/BF16`, `512 FLOP/clock/CU` FP16 on `XTX` vs `256` on `6950 XT`) [gpuopen.com/learn/wmma_on_rdna3](https://gpuopen.com/learn/wmma_on_rdna3). But `llama.cpp`'s HIP Flash was removed (`#26046`), ROCm `10.0.0` docs list only *Instinct* `MI300` in the spec table and `Composible Kernel` as default FA2 backend [rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/composable_kernel/en/latest](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/composable_kernel/en/latest) — no `gfx1100` `8k/16k/32k` `tok/s` number exists. The `XT`'s `800 GB/s` pin caps prefill: `1000·15.3 GB ≈15.3 TB/s` of memory movement if naive — impossible. Even fused `1–2×` model-size traffic still needs `>15 TB/s`. Realistic fused ceiling with perfect WMMA is **500–800 tok/s**, not 1000, without sparsity/multi-GPU.
*   **Custom kernels are the only headroom — and the last measured delta was 1.178× not 1.2× under DXG jitter.** Prior Phase 7 kernels hit `1.178×` (`attn_q 111.47→94.67 µs`, avg `1.00×`) vs real `vec_dot_iq4_xs_q8_1` DP4A. The shortfall is the virtualization tax: WSL2 adds `16 GiB` invisible `DXGI/Hyper-V` overhead where `mem_get_info` reports `80+ GiB` free yet `3.48 GiB` contiguous alloc fails [github.com/microsoft/WSL/issues/40401](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL/issues/40401) / `BSOD` after `3–5` OOMs corrupts GPU-PV translation [github.com/microsoft/WSL/issues/40732](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL/issues/40732), plus `15–30 µs` extra launch latency that swallows `<2%` micro-opts, and **no `rocprofv3`/`librocdxg` counters at all** on WSL2 (docs 404, compat matrix `Instinct-only`) — wall-clock only.
*   **Bottom line recipe (if you insist on 8k):** you need *all* of: (a) `Fa2` via `Composable Kernel`/`aiter` Triton autotune `BLOCK_M 128/BLOCK_N 64` *on* `gfx1100`, (b) `GQA 4×` KV saving + optional `int4 KV` (`~2.5×`, but `3×` slowdown without optimized `quanto` kernels), (c) `b ≥2048` (`-b 2048`, `ubatch 512`, `-ctk f16 -ctv f16`, `--flash-attn on`) [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/tools/llama-bench/README.md](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/tools/llama-bench/README.md), (d) **fused dequant+WMMA** `64×32` double-buffered LDS `[2][32][33]` (`+1` padding to kill `32-bank` conflicts), `wave32`, `__launch_bounds__(256,4)` + `__builtin_amdgcn_wmma_f32_16x16x16_f16_w32` [gpuopen.com/learn/wmma_on_rdna3](https://gpuopen.com/learn/wmma_on_rdna3) [rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/HIP/en/latest/how-to/hip_cpp_language_extensions.html](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/HIP/en/latest/how-to/hip_cpp_language_extensions.html), `sudot4` DP4A path, replicated lanes `0–15→16–31`, and (e) **bare-metal Linux** (native HIP SDK, `clang++ --offload-arch=gfx1100`, `-HSA_ENABLE_DXG_DETECTION=1` off) — because **WSL2 cannot profile or reliably measure the win**.

**If you keep WSL2, cap at `4k–6k` or switch to native Windows/Linux.** `1000 tok/s at 8k` on `gfx1100` via WSL2 is aspirational until AMD ships a supported `gfx1100` Flash/CK path and Microsoft fixes DXG contiguous-alloc lying.

## Key Findings

### 1. Prefill scaling — why 8k halves you even with Flash

1. **Attention = `O(N²·d)` FLOPs, Flash = `O(N)` HBM.** `FlashAttention-1` fuses `QKᵀ→softmax→PV` in SRAM tiles, cutting HBM from quadratic to linear (`2–4×` over optimized unfused) — but compute bound → `25–40%` of GEMM peak. `FlashAttention-2` re-parallelizes a single head across `threadblock/warp` (`reduce non-matmul FLOPs, reduce shared-mem`) → `~2×` over FA1, `50–73%` GEMM on `A100`, `225 TFLOP/s` E2E [arxiv 2307.08691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08691) [arxiv 2205.14135](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14135) [huggingface.co/docs/text-generation-inference/conceptual/flash_attention](https://huggingface.co/docs/text-generation-inference/conceptual/flash_attention).
2. **`tok/s = N / time(N)` — even fused, `time ∝ N²`.** `HBM` linear shifts bottleneck to `FLOPs` (`SRAM 128×128` tiles limit writes, not multiplies). `8k→16k→32k` is `2×/4×` latency *with* Flash, `~16×` without. `DaM` → `A100` keeps `>50%` GEMM longer so `8k→32k` is `3–4×` not `16×`; without Flash it is `2–4×` slower *or OOM*. No gfx1100 curve exists — extrapolated from `A100` [github.com/Dao-AILab/flash-attention](https://github.com/Dao-AILab/flash-attention) [rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/composable_kernel/en/latest](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/composable_kernel/en/latest).
3. **Why 8k is a cliff on consumer:** first `N` where (i) `KV` alone crosses VRAM, (ii) `FLOPs ≫` token-embed GEMMs (`67M` elements/head at 8k vs `4M` at 2k → `16×` per layer), (iii) `llama.cpp` `ggml` graph (`10–20` kernels/layer + CPU dispatch) loses to one fused Flash launch. Historically `gfx1100` had no Flash (`HIP rocWMMA Flash removed #26046`), falling back to unfused `ggml` — `8k prefill` collapses.
4. **`llama.cpp GEMM threshold M≥128`.** Prefill issues `QK`/`PV` as GEMMs with `M=seq_len`. GEMM is tensor-core/wave-occupancy efficient when `M≥128`; `decode M=1` is bandwidth-bound needing Flash. At `8k`, `M=8192` roofs to GEMM peak — the remaining loss is the `ggml` graph and extra `softmax` HBM passes. `CK` `mmq` kernels autotune `BLOCK_M 128/BLOCK_N 64` for `M≥128` but non-Flash still does `softmax` roundtrips. Metal Flash tuning, `Vulkan 30→67 tok/s Mesa 26.1.7` on `Arc B580`, and queue `#5000` show backends are still maturing [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp) [github.com/ggml-org/ggml](https://github.com/ggml-org/ggml) [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/discussions](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/discussions) [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/issues/5000](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/issues/5000).

### 2. KV & GQA/MQA — the VRAM math that sets your cap

5. **Per-token formula from HF:** `per_token = 2·(K+V)·2B(fp16)·layers·kv_heads·head_dim`. `HF blog` example: `Llama-2 7B` (`32L/32H/128`) ⇒ `10k tokens→5 GB ⇒ 0.5 MB/tok (512 KiB)` [huggingface.co/blog/kv-cache-quantization](https://huggingface.co/blog/kv-cache-quantization). With GQA (`32 Q /8 KV`): `2·2·32·8·128=131072 B ≈128 KiB/tok` fp16. `Qwen3-32B` card confirms `64 layers, GQA 64 Q/8 KV, 32,768 native /131,072 YaRN` [huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-32B](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-32B). So `Qwen3.8-27B` (`~28–32L`, `GQA 8–16 KV`) ⇒ `64–256 KiB/tok fp16`, `32–128 KiB int4` (`~2.5×` saving measured [huggingface.co/blog/kv-cache-quantization](https://huggingface.co/blog/kv-cache-quantization)). Thus `8k×64KiB=512 MB`, `8k×128KiB=1 GB`, `8k×512KiB=4 GB`, `32k⇒2–16 GB`. Reference `RX 7900 XTX` `96 CU, 96 MB L3` vs `6950 XT 128 MB` [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RDNA_3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RDNA_3) — `8k KV` (`3.2 GB` for `32L`) dwarfs every cache.
6. **`GQA = 4× saving, MQA =32×, near-free.** `Shazeer 1911.02150` (`MQA: 32→1 ⇒32×` KV/BW, decode win, tiny quality loss) [arxiv 1911.02150](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.02150) → `Ainslie 2305.13245` (`GQA: 32→8 ⇒4×`, uptrained from `MHA` with `5%` pretrain to near-`MHA` quality, `~MQA` speed) [arxiv 2305.13245](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13245) [huggingface.co/blog/kv-cache-quantization](https://huggingface.co/blog/kv-cache-quantization). `Qwen` uses `GQA 4–8` — without it `8k` before OOM is impossible.
7. **`int4 KV` extends context `2.5×` but with `3×` slowdown today.** HF `quanto` `int4` per-token `residual=128` holds `PG-19/LongBench` perplexity (`int4≈fp16`, `int2` collapses `SAMSum 41→14, TriviaQA 84→63`); `weight+KV int4` reaches `128k` on `80 GB A100` with Flash vs `40k fp16`. But every step quant/dequant + absent `quanto` kernels = `3×` slower at batch `>1`. For `7900 XT Q4 + int4 KV` similar `2×` extension but `hip` `dequant` overhead matters [huggingface.co/blog/kv-cache-quantization](https://huggingface.co/blog/kv-cache-quantization).

### 3. llama-bench baseline — what `1000 tok/s` actually means

8. **`pp ≫ tg` by `10–55×`.** `llama-bench` README: `pp (prompt processing/prefill)` vs `tg (text generation)` `tok/s` excludes tokenize/sample. `llama 7B Q4_0` `tg128≈132 t/s` vs `pp1024≈2498 t/s` [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/tools/llama-bench/README.md](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/tools/llama-bench/README.md).
9. **Concrete ceiling:** `qwen2 7B Q4_K Medium pp512 = 7340±23 t/s` on `RTX 4080 CUDA` full offload (`-b 512`, drops `~12%` to `6425 t/s` with `d512`) [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/tools/llama-bench/README.md](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/tools/llama-bench/README.md) — `27–32B` at `8k` is `4–5×` params *and* `16×` quadratic → `1000 tok/s` is already aggressive.
10. **Batch/offload knobs dominate.** `pp1024` jumps `1436→2498 t/s` when `-b 128→1024`; `-ngl 10:373 → -ngl 35:2400 t/s` (`pp512`) — partial offload kills prefill. `-b` default `2048`, `ubatch 512`, `-ctk/ctv`, `--flash-attn auto/on/off` (−fa) are the `8k` tunables [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/tools/llama-bench/README.md](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/tools/llama-bench/README.md). `AGENTS.md` mandates `-b 2048` at `≥4096` and `--single-turn --simple-io --load-mode none` to avoid buffer panes.
11. **`Gated DeltaNet/Mamba2` not found.** `arxiv 2312.06648` returned `Dense X Retrieval` (granularity, not DeltaNet) [arxiv 2312.06648](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06648) — no `Qwen 27B` `gfx1100` linear-recurrence vs full-attention `8k/32k` numbers available; expect `~2×` at `32k+` `O(N)` but with recall tradeoffs, unmeasured here.

### 4. RDNA3 gfx1100 WMMA — the hardware you must fuse into

12. **Single WMMA = 32 clocks of optimal scheduling.** GPUOpen: *"A single WMMA instruction coordinates 32 clocks of optimal work scheduling … source reuse, forwarding, minimal VGPR access"* [gpuopen.com/learn/wmma_on_rdna3](https://gpuopen.com/learn/wmma_on_rdna3).
13. **Throughput table (RX 7900 XTX):** `FP16 512 FLOP/clock/CU`, `BF16 512` (vs `RDNA2 256`), `INT8 512`, `INT4 1024` [gpuopen.com/learn/wmma_on_rdna3](https://gpuopen.com/learn/wmma_on_rdna3) — these are *wave-cooperative* rates, not per-lane `VALU`. `~96 CU ×2.5 GHz ×512 ≈122 TFLOP/s FP16` peak (Wikipedia quotes `61.4 TFLOP FP32 / 122.8 TFLOP FP16` [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RDNA_3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RDNA_3)); sustained needs `~75%` via fused dequant+WMMA.
14. **Wave-cooperative tile:** `16×16×16 (MxNxK)`, only `16×16` on `RDNA3` [gpuopen.com/learn/wmma_on_rdna3](https://gpuopen.com/learn/wmma_on_rdna3). Layout: `A` column-major packed, `B/C/D` row-major, `C/D` unpacked, `OPSEL` for `f16/bf16` half. `A_frag/B_frag` `8 VGPR` `fp16/bf16` (`4` `iu8`, `2` `iu4`), `C/D` `8 VGPR wave32` / `4 VGPR wave64`.
15. **Lane replication tax:** `wave32` replicates `lanes 0–15→16–31` (two copies), `wave64` `0–15→32–47/48–63` (four copies) [gpuopen.com/learn/wmma_on_rdna3](https://gpuopen.com/learn/wmma_on_rdna3) — `wave32` is native sweet spot for `8k` GEMMs.
16. **12 intrinsics:** `w32/w64 × {f32_f16, f16_f16, bf16_bf16, i32_iu8, i32_iu4, ...}` with `D_frag = __builtin_amdgcn_wmma_<C,D>_16x16x16_<A,B>_w<32|64>(A,B,C,OPSEL)` [gpuopen.com/learn/wmma_on_rdna3](https://gpuopen.com/learn/wmma_on_rdna3).
17. **Build:** `hipcc --offload-arch=gfx1100` (ROCm `5.4+/6.4+`, `rocWMMA` supports `gfx1100/1101/1102/115x/120x` [github.com/ROCm/rocWMMA/blob/develop/README.md](https://github.com/ROCm/rocWMMA/blob/develop/README.md)), `wave32` via `dim3(32)` and `__launch_bounds__(256,4)` + `amdgpu` waves [rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/HIP/en/latest/how-to/hip_cpp_language_extensions.html](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/HIP/en/latest/how-to/hip_cpp_language_extensions.html).
18. **ROCm path:** `rocWMMA` is header-only `C++` templated, coop fragment decomposition, `CUDA WMMA`-compatible [rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/rocWMMA/en/latest](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/rocWMMA/en/latest) [rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/rocWMMA/en/latest/what-is-rocwmma.html](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/rocWMMA/en/latest/what-is-rocwmma.html) vs `CDNA MFMA`. `pingou` on consumer `RDNA` ⇒ use `WMMA`, not `MFMA`.

### 5. `Q8_1` + DP4A + IQ4_XS — why fused beats float by `≥4×`

19. **`Q8_1` = `4×` cut.** `block_q8_1 = {half d; half s (=sum); qs[32]=int8} =36B/32 =1.125 B/elem` (`fp32 128 B/32`) [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-common.h](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-common.h). `quantize_mmq_q8_1_cuda` quantizes `M=8k` activations on-the-fly into `src1_q8_1` (`ds half2(d,d*sum)`), feeding `int8` DP4A without `fp32` materialization [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/mmq.cu](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/mmq.cu) [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/quantize.cu](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/quantize.cu).
20. **DP4A does 4 dots in one.** `common.cuh: GGML_CUDA_CC_DP4A=610 → __dp4a(a,b,c)` (CUDA) / `__builtin_amdgcn_sdot4 / sudot4` (HIP: `CDNA/RDNA2` `sdot`, `RDNA3/4` `sudot4(true,...)`) [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/common.cuh](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/common.cuh). `VDR_Q4_0_Q8_1_MMQ=4`: two `dp4a` per `32` elems = `16` `INT8 MAC`s [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/vecdotq.cuh](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/vecdotq.cuh) [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/mmq-vec-dot.cuh](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/mmq-vec-dot.cuh).
21. **`IQ4_XS` decode:** `136B/256=4.25 bpw`, `4b` non-linear `kvalues_iq4nl [-127…113]` [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-common.h](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-common.h) [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/dequantize.cuh](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/dequantize.cuh). `vec_dot_iq4_xs_q8_1: ls=(scales_l…)+(scales_h…), scale=ls-32, d=half2float(bq4->d)*low2float(aq_ds), get_int_from_table_16(aux_q4,kvalues) → int2 v, u=get_int_b4(aq.qs), sumi=dp4a(v.x,u0)+dp4a(v.y,u1), *= (ls-32)*d` — fused after DP4A, no `half` convert until final [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/vecdotq.cuh](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/vecdotq.cuh).
22. **Fused vs naive:** `ggml_cuda_mul_mat_q()` fuses `quantize_mmq_q8_1_cuda + ggml_cuda_mmq`; naive dequantizes `IQ4_XS→FP16` (`~30 MB` per `5120×6144` mat) then `cuBLAS/TF32` — `2×` HBM passes + `8×` bandwidth [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/mmq.cu](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/mmq.cu).
23. **Dispatch:** `MMQ` (`M` batched, prefill) vs `MMVQ` (`M=1` decode, `VDR 1–2`, `MMVQ_MAX_BATCH_SIZE=8`) [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/mmvq.cu](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/mmvq.cu). `ggml_cuda_should_use_mmq()` checks `type/cc/ne11/n_experts`; `mmq-config-rdna3.cuh` picks tiles (`J=128/256` etc) [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/mmq-config-rdna3.cuh](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/mmq-config-rdna3.cuh). ## Detailed Analysis

### How prefill *should* scale and what Flash changes

*Algebra:* `tok/s = N / (a·N² + b·N + c)` (`a` = `QKᵀ/PV` FMAs, `b` = `WQKV` GEMMTF, `c` = graph). Unfused: `HBM` is `O(N²)` (materialized `N×N`), so `a` dominates both `FLOPs` and bytes → naive `67M·64≈4.3B` elements at `8k` vs `270 B` at `32k` — `16×`. Tiled Flash keeps `Q/K/V` in `LDS`/`SRAM` (`BLOCK_M 128/BLOCK_N 64` autotuned in `aiter`/`CK` [github.com/Dao-AILab/flash-attention](https://github.com/Dao-AILab/flash-attention) [rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/composable_kernel/en/latest](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/composable_kernel/en/latest)): `HBM` linear (`load once, fused, write once` [huggingface.co/docs/text-generation-inference/conceptual/flash_attention](https://huggingface.co/docs/text-generation-inference/conceptual/flash_attention)). Gap to GEMM shrinks `25–40%→50–73%` → `A100 3–4×` not `16×` for `8k→32k`. On `gfx1100` the fallback unfused `ggml` attention is un-tiled → `8k` is `2–4×` slower than FA or OOM.

### The concrete `8k` budget

```
Model IQ4_XS: 15.31 GB
KV fp16:      8k ×64 KiB ≈ 0.5 GB … 8k×128 KiB≈1.0 GB … 8k×512 KiB≈4.0 GB  (GQA spread)
              For Qwen32B-like (64L, 8KV, 128 hd): 8k× (2·2·64·8·128)= 8k×262 KiB≈2.1 GB fp16
              int4 KV:       ~0.85 GB (2.5× saving, +3× launch cost)
Attention buf: 67M fp16 ≈134 MB/head-group (exceeds 96 MB L3)
Transient:    + rope/norm/graph 0.3–0.5 GB
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8k total:    ~16.7–19.9 GB on 20 GB → 0.1–3.3 GB slack
32k total:   ~28 GB → OOM, spills to host via .wslconfig memory=24GB swap, thrash → <200 tok/s
```

With `GQA 4×` the curve moves right by `4×` — that's the only reason `8k` is *possible*.

### Where `1000 tok/s` comes from arithmetically

`27B` `W4A8` prefill `FLOPs ≈2·27e9·N` (`2·K·N` per token per layer, approx). `N=8192 ⇒ ~440 TFLOP` per full forward (weights streamed once per layer). `RX 7900 XTX` `122 TFLOP/s` FP16 peak ⇒ `~3.6 s` ideal ⇒ `2275 tok/s` in vacuum. Real: `WMMA 512 FLOP/clock/CU ≈61 TFLOP/s` sustained per `CU` mix, `DRAM` bound, instruction overhead, `rope/softmax/norm`, `DXG` copies, imperfect tiling. `50–73%` GEMM `⇒ 500–800 tok/s` ceiling. `1000` needs `~20–30 TFLOP/s` *sustained* at `8k` *with* fused path — creatable only if every GEMM is `WMMA` and attention is fully `CK`/Triton-fused.

### Why the naive path loses at `8k` and the fused path still struggles

*Unfused* dequant→`FP16`→`cublas/hiplAS` does `2×` HBM passes + `8×` weight bandwidth. `Fused` keeps `4b` in `L1`, `Q8_1` in `SRAM`. The remaining `8k` losses are: (i) unfused `softmax` passes (extra `HBM` roundtrips), (ii) `ggml` graph `CPU` scheduling (`triple chevron <<< >>>` / `hipLaunchKernelGGL` [HIP lang ext](https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/HIP/en/latest/how-to/hip_cpp_language_extensions.html)), (iii) `DXG` shared-mem translation, (iv) `L2 6MB / L3 96MB` ≪ `134 MB` attention tile ⇒ res- streaming from `GDDR6`. That's why `Metal 30→67 tok/s` and `Vulkan` gains came from Flash-like fusion, not GEMM tuning alone [github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/discussions](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/discussions).

### Scheduling & waves

`llvm.amdgcn.sched_barrier 0x0008` blocks `MFMA/WMMA` reordering across barriers; `sched_group_barrier(mask,size,syncID)` pipelines `DS↔WMMA` [llvm.org/docs/AMDGPUUsage.html](https://llvm.org/docs/AMDGPUUsage.html). `wave32` + `__launch_bounds__(256,4)` ensures `8 wave32/block`, `4 warps/EU` ⇒ `~512 thr/CU` to hide `LDS` latency; `wave64` would give `4 VGPR` vs `8` for `C/D` but `2×` duplication overhead and lower wave count, worse for latency-hiding prefill.
