**ROCm/DXG (guest):**
- ROCm 7.2.1 + librocdxg 1.2.2 over `/dev/dxg`; driver 32.0.31041.1004 (frozen; WMI fingerprint + ENV gates re-run on drift, D-04 no-silent-updates policy)

**llama.cpp integration (anti-fork quilt):**
- Patch: `patches/0001-gfx1100-mul-mat-custom.patch` (356 lines / 276 insertions; `git apply --check` PASS with `core.autocrlf=false` + `*.patch eol=lf` seen in `.gitattributes`)
- Dispatch hooks: `llama.cpp/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/mmvq.cu:1280` (GEMV, M=1 IQ4_XS) and `mmq.cu:114` (GEMM, M≥16 IQ4_XS), both guarded by `GGML_CUDA_ENABLE_CUSTOM_GFX1100` (default OFF — bit-identical to upstream when OFF)
- Vendored headers: `llama.cpp/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/custom_gfx1100/gemv_iq4xs.cuh`, `gemm_iq4xs.cuh`, `empty.cuh` (stub) — sourced from `kernels/matmul_iq4xs/impl_gemv_dp4a_gfx1100.hip` + `impl_gemm_wmma_stream.hip`
- GGML layout contract: weights `[K,N]` blocks, activations `X[gm*K+gk]` ([K,M]), output `Y[m*N+n]` ([N,M]→[M,N] fix)

**Kernel playground isolation:**
